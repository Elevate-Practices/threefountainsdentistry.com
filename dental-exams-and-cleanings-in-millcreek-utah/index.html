Why routine visits matter

Most serious dental problems, like cavities, gum disease and cracked teeth, start small and quiet. A routine exam and cleaning is how we catch them while they're still simple and inexpensive to treat, instead of waiting until something hurts.

It's also just good maintenance: a professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing alone can't fully clear.

What's in a visit

  • A full exam of your teeth, gums and bite by Dr. Cannon or Dr. Johansen
  • A professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar
  • Digital X-rays when needed, to catch what's below the surface
  • Fluoride to help strengthen enamel
  • A straight conversation about anything we see. No surprise treatment plans.

How often should you come in?

Most people do best with a checkup and cleaning every six months. If you have a history of gum disease or cavities, more frequent visits may help, and we'll talk about it with you directly if so.

Nervous? Bringing a kid?

Totally common. We walk you through everything before we do it and go at a pace that works for you.

Questions people actually ask

How long does an exam and cleaning take?

Most routine visits take about 45 minutes to an hour, depending on whether X-rays are needed and how much buildup there is.

Is a cleaning painful?

For most people, no. If your teeth or gums are sensitive, tell us first and we'll adjust to keep you comfortable.

When should kids start coming in?

By their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth. If your child is older and hasn't been seen yet, it's never too late to start.

Does insurance cover exams and cleanings?

Most dental plans cover routine exams and cleanings at little or no cost to you. No insurance? Our membership plan covers them at 100%.
