Questions people actually ask

Is this the same as dental insurance?

No. It's an in-house savings plan, not insurance. No insurance company, no claims, no waiting for approval. You pay one yearly fee and your covered services are free or discounted at your visit.

Can I use it with dental insurance?

The plan is designed for people without dental insurance. If you have insurance, call us at (801) 261-2013 and we'll help you figure out which option makes more sense.

When does it start working?

Immediately. No waiting period. You can use your benefits the day you enroll.

What happens when it expires?

Plans run one year from your purchase date. We'll let you know when it's time to renew.

What's the difference between Dual and Family?

Dual covers two people: a parent and child, or two spouses. Family covers up to six family members (children up to age 19); more members are $125 each.

Ready to enroll?

Give us a call and we'll get you set up. Most people are enrolled and using their benefits within minutes.
