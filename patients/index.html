New to Three Fountains?

Here's the honest version.

Your first visit is low-key. No lecture, no upsell. Just a real exam, a conversation about what you actually need, and a team that's genuinely glad you're here.

  1. 1

    Before you arrive

    Bring a photo ID and your insurance card if you have one. Arrive a few minutes early so we can get your paperwork done.

  2. 2

    Your exam

    A full exam, any X-rays you need, and a straight conversation about what we see. No pressure, no surprise treatment plans.

  3. 3

    Your plan

    If you need treatment, we walk through options and costs together before anything's scheduled. Nothing happens that you didn't agree to.

Insurance

We do the paperwork. You do the smiling.

We work with nearly all major dental insurance plans and file claims for you. Not sure your plan's accepted? Call us and we'll check before you book.

Most plans cover part of standard treatment, but cosmetic work usually isn't covered. Partial benefits sometimes apply if a tooth already has a filling, crown or other work. We always confirm your coverage before treatment, not after.
