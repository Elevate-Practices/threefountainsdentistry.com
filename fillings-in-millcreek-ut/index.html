When you need a filling

A cavity is a small hole in your tooth caused by decay. Left alone, it gets bigger and can reach the nerve. A filling stops it in its tracks.

Signs to watch for

  • Sensitivity to sweets, hot or cold
  • A dark spot or hole you can see or feel
  • Food that keeps getting stuck in one place
  • A twinge when you bite down

How it works

We numb the area, clean out the decay, and fill the space with a tooth-colored material shaped to fit your bite. Most fillings take under an hour, and you can eat normally once the numbness wears off.

Questions people actually ask

Do fillings hurt?

We numb the area first, so you should feel pressure but not pain.

How long do fillings last?

With good care, many fillings last for years. We'll check them at your regular visits.

Does insurance cover fillings?

Most plans cover part of the cost. Coverage for tooth-colored fillings on back teeth varies by plan, and we'll confirm yours before treatment.
