Questions people actually ask

How long does a smile makeover take?

Anywhere from one visit to several months, depending on what's in your plan.

Can I do it in stages?

Yes. Lots of people spread treatment out to fit their schedule and budget.

Does insurance cover a smile makeover?

Cosmetic treatments usually aren't covered, but some parts, like crowns that repair damage, may be. Members save 20% on all cosmetic dentistry.
