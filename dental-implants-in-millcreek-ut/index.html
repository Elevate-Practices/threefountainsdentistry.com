Dental implants in Millcreek, UT

An implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up, so it looks, feels and chews like the real thing.

Book now (801) 261-2013

What's a dental implant?

An implant is a small titanium post that sits in your jawbone where a tooth root used to be. Once it heals, a custom crown goes on top. The result is a stable, natural-looking tooth that doesn't slide around or need to come out at night.

Why people choose implants

  • Replace one tooth without touching the teeth next to it
  • Chew and smile with confidence
  • Help keep your jawbone healthy where a tooth is missing
  • Stabilize dentures so they stop slipping

How it works

Implants happen in a few stages over several months: planning, placing the post, letting it heal and bond with the bone, then attaching your crown. We'll explain exactly how your treatment will work, how long it takes and what it costs before anything starts.

Questions people actually ask

Am I a candidate for an implant?

Most healthy adults with a missing tooth are. An exam and X-rays tell us whether you have enough bone and healthy gums for an implant.

How long do implants last?

With good care, an implant can last for many years, often decades. The crown on top may need replacing over time.

Does insurance cover implants?

Some plans cover part of the cost. We'll check your coverage, and members save 20%.
