Straighten your teeth with clear, removable aligners. Most people won't even notice you're wearing them.

Book now (801) 261-2013

How clear aligners work

You wear a series of custom clear trays, switching to a new set every week or two. Each set moves your teeth a little closer to where they should be. You take them out to eat, brush and floss.

What they can fix

  • Crowded teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Teeth that shifted after braces
  • Many overbites, underbites and crossbites

Questions people actually ask

How long does treatment take?

It depends on your teeth. Some cases take a few months, others longer. We'll give you an estimate after your scan.

Do aligners hurt?

You may feel pressure for a day or two with each new set. That means they're working.

How many hours a day do I wear them?

Most of the day and night, usually 20 to 22 hours. You take them out to eat and drink anything but water.
